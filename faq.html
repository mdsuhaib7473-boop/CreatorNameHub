⚖️ Trademark & Legal

Yes. Any name generated by CreatorNameHub is yours to use. We do not claim any ownership over the suggestions the AI produces. However, ownership of a name in a business or legal sense requires you to register it as a trademark or business entity — AI-generated suggestions are not automatically legally protected.

Yes, if you plan to use your name for a serious business or long-term creative project, we strongly recommend registering a trademark. Trademark protection gives you the exclusive legal right to use the name in your industry and prevents others from copying it. In most countries, you can apply for a trademark through your national intellectual property office. In the USA, this is the USPTO. In India, it is IP India. In the UK, it is the UKIPO.

CreatorNameHub does not currently perform real-time trademark database searches. We strongly recommend that you conduct your own trademark search before committing to any name. Check your country's official trademark database — USPTO.gov for the US, ipindia.gov.in for India — and consider consulting a legal professional for important business names.

Before committing to any name, we recommend checking:

  • Domain availability (especially .com)
  • Social media handle availability (Instagram, YouTube, Twitter, TikTok)
  • Trademark databases in your country
  • Google search for existing businesses with the same name
  • Any negative meanings in other languages if targeting international audiences
